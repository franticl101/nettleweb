Every mania has a first hour

Tulips in Haarlem in 1637. Mississippi shares on the rue Quincampoix in 1720. South Sea stock in London that same summer. In every print of those crowds, the people in the picture arrived after the first hour.

Memecoins run the same play in minutes. Thousands of tokens launch on Solana every day, and most of what decides a launch happens in its first block: who bought, through how many wallets, and what the dev kept the power to do.

The chart shows none of that. It shows price, and price is the last thing to move. In October 1929 the ticker on the New York floor ran hours behind the trading. A chart on a new token is the same tape, running late.

So we built what we wanted on our own screens: a reader that opens every launch the moment it lands on chain and says, in plain words, what it found. It doesn’t trade for you, and it never asks for your keys. It reads, so you can decide.

We’re letting people in a small group at a time. If you trade launches, we’d like you in one.

How you use it

  1. Paste an address, or let it watch every launch.

    Drop in a contract address from anywhere, or turn on the feed and Block Zero opens each new pump.fun, PumpSwap and Raydium token as it lands.

  2. Then it reads the first block, and keeps reading.

    Authorities, bundled buys, snipers, the dev’s past launches, the top holders and your own wallet list, updated as new buyers come in.

  3. The verdict lands where you trade.

    A short report in the app and the same report in Telegram, with each flag said in one line and the address ready to copy.
